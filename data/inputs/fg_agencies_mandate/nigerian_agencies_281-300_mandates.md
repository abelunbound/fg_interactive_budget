(g) Conduct complex surgical procedures including general surgery, orthopedics and gynecological surgeries
(h) Provide comprehensive maternal and child health services including high-risk obstetrics
(i) Operate intensive care units and high-dependency units for critically ill patients
(j) Conduct diagnostic services including pathology, radiology, ultrasound and medical imaging
(k) Provide cancer diagnosis and treatment including chemotherapy
(l) Offer dialysis services for chronic kidney disease patients
(m) Conduct medical research advancing healthcare knowledge and practice
(n) Provide postgraduate medical training and residency programmes for specialist certification
(o) Offer continuing medical education for healthcare professionals
(p) Serve as apex referral hospital for southern Benue State and environs
(q) Conduct public health programmes and disease surveillance

(s) Maintain teaching hospital accreditation standards for medical education
