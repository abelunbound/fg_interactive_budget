# NIGERIAN FEDERAL AGENCY MANDATES: AGENCIES 351-380

## 351. FGGC EFON ALAYE

**MDA Code:** 517026052

Federal Government Girls College Efon Alaye, Ekiti State, is a federal girls-only unity school located in Efon Alaye, Ekiti State in South-West Nigeria. Established as part of Federal Government Girls Colleges system to promote national unity and girls' education, the school provides secondary education exclusively for female students from JSS1 to SS3. Operating under Federal Ministry of Education, FGGC Efon Alaye admits girls from across Nigeria through National Common Entrance Examination using 60% merit and 40% quota system. The school provides boarding facilities bringing together girls from diverse ethnic, religious and socio-economic backgrounds. FGGC Efon Alaye promotes women empowerment and national integration in Ekiti State context.

**Core Mandate:** To provide quality secondary education for girls promoting national unity, women empowerment and academic excellence in Ekiti State.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Provide secondary education exclusively for female students from JSS1 to SS3 aged 10-18 years
(b) Admit girls nationally through Common Entrance Examination ensuring geographical spread across all states
(c) Implement 60% merit-based and 40% quota-based admission system for female students
(d) Promote national unity by bringing together girls from all states, ethnic groups and religious backgrounds
(e) Provide girls-only boarding facilities with appropriate security, safety measures and gender-sensitive infrastructure
(f) Deliver Nigerian national curriculum prescribed by Federal Ministry of Education with gender-sensitive pedagogical approach
(g) Prepare girls for WAEC (WASSCE) and NECO (SSCE) examinations
(h) Provide co-curricular activities promoting girls' holistic development including sports, arts, drama, debates and clubs
(i) Maintain high academic standards befitting Federal Unity School status
(j) Employ qualified teachers including female role models, mentors and counselors
(k) Provide educational infrastructure including science laboratories, libraries, ICT facilities and workshops suitable for girls
(l) Foster inter-ethnic, inter-religious and inter-regional understanding among female students
(m) Develop girls' leadership skills, confidence, self-esteem, assertiveness and public speaking abilities
(n) Provide character, moral, civic and values education addressing girls' development needs
(o) Ensure safe, secure learning environment addressing girls' specific safety, health and privacy needs
(p) Provide healthcare services including reproductive health education, menstrual hygiene management and counseling
(q) Implement school feeding programme for boarding students ensuring nutrition
(r) Organize inter-house sports competitions, academic competitions and talent development programmes
(s) Prepare girls for university entrance examinations (JAMB/UTME) and career development
(t) Empower girls as future leaders for national development, unity and women advancement in Nigeria

## 379. FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY COMMISSION

**MDA Code:** 250001001

The Fiscal Responsibility Commission (FRC) was established by Fiscal Responsibility Act 2007 (Act No. 31) to ensure implementation of its provisions promoting prudent management of Nigeria's resources. The Commission operates under the Presidency with mandate to promote transparent and accountable government financial management framework. FRC enforces fiscal discipline, monitors public expenditure, ensures macro-economic stability and promotes economic objectives in Section 16 of Constitution. The Commission monitors Medium-Term Expenditure Framework (MTEF), Excess Crude Account, debt management and budget implementation ensuring fiscal prudence across federal, state and local governments.

**Core Mandate:** To monitor and enforce fiscal responsibility provisions ensuring prudent management of public resources, transparency in fiscal operations and macro-economic stability.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Monitor and enforce provisions of Fiscal Responsibility Act 2007 promoting Constitutional economic objectives
(b) Disseminate standard fiscal practices including international good practices for efficient public expenditure management
(c) Undertake fiscal and financial studies, analysis and diagnosis disseminating results publicly
(d) Monitor operation of Medium-Term Expenditure Framework (MTEF) ensuring compliance
(e) Monitor Excess Crude Account operations and Nigerian Sovereign Investment Authority funds
(f) Ensure revenue-raising policies, resource allocation and debt management decisions are undertaken prudently
(g) Promote transparency and accountability in fiscal operations at all government levels
(h) Monitor federal government compliance with fiscal targets and budget discipline
(i) Compel any person or government institution to disclose information on public revenues and expenditure
(j) Investigate violations of Fiscal Responsibility Act provisions
(k) Forward investigation reports to Attorney-General for prosecution of offenders
(l) Monitor public debt sustainability and borrowing limits compliance
(m) Promote long-term macro-economic stability of national economy
(n) Monitor revenue collection efficiency and expenditure patterns
(o) Ensure budget implementation follows appropriation and cash plan
(p) Promote fiscal federalism and inter-governmental fiscal coordination
(q) Monitor states and local governments fiscal responsibility adoption
(r) Submit annual reports to National Assembly on fiscal compliance
(s) Make rules for carrying out Commission functions
(t) Operate independently in performing functions without interference
