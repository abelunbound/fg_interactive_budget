## 861. UNIVERSITY OF ABUJA TEACHING HOSPITAL, GWAGWALADA

**MDA Code:** 521026015

(k) Provide obstetrics and gynecology services
(l) Provide emergency and trauma care
(m) Conduct medical research and clinical trials
(n) Operate modern diagnostic and treatment facilities
(o) Collaborate with University of Abuja College of Medicine
(p) Train nurses, pharmacists and allied health professionals
(q) Provide community health outreach programs

## 880. UPPER BENUE RIVER BASIN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY

**MDA Code:** 252047001

Upper Benue River Basin Development Authority manages water resources in Upper Benue basin. Operates under Federal Ministry of Water Resources as one of 12 RBDAs. Covers northeastern states including Adamawa, Taraba and parts of Borno and Gombe. Develops irrigation schemes. Operates dams and reservoirs. Manages Benue River upper catchment.

**Core Mandate:** To manage Upper Benue basin water resources, develop irrigation, control floods and support agriculture in northeastern Nigeria.

**Statutory Functions:**

(e) Operate in Borno State areas
(f) Operate in Gombe State areas
(g) Provide water for agriculture
(h) Provide water for livestock
(i) Provide water for domestic use
(j) Develop dry season farming through irrigation
(k) Support rice production
(l) Support maize production
(m) Support vegetable production
(n) Operate dams and reservoirs in basin
(o) Control flooding in Benue catchments
(p) Provide water supply infrastructure for communities
(q) Support pastoral livestock watering
(r) Manage river basin environmental protection
(s) Conduct water resources assessment and planning
(t) Advance northeastern water management supporting agriculture through irrigation development and Benue basin management
