## 821. PRESIDENTIAL TECHNICAL COMMITTEE ON LAND REFORMS

**MDA Code:** 161019001

Presidential Technical Committee on Land Reforms is expert panel advising President on land tenure, land administration and land reform policies. Operates under Office of President. Addresses Nigeria's land ownership system including Land Use Act 1978 which vests all land in state governors. Reviews multiple land tenure systems including statutory, customary and religious frameworks. Examines land title registration, Certificates of Occupancy issuance and cadastral survey systems.

**Core Mandate:** To review land laws and policies, recommend land tenure reforms, address land administration challenges and support secure land rights.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Review Nigerian land laws and land tenure systems
(b) Assess Land Use Act 1978 implementation and impacts
(c) Recommend land tenure reforms and policy changes
(d) Address land administration inefficiencies and corruption
(e) Propose solutions to land title registration challenges
(f) Review multiple land tenure systems (statutory, customary, religious)
(g) Address conflicts between statutory and customary land rights
(h) Recommend agricultural land access improvements
(i) Address urban land speculation and housing affordability
(j) Propose solutions to land grabbing and fake titles
(k) Review Certificates of Occupancy issuance processes
(l) Address boundary disputes and land conflicts
(m) Recommend cadastral survey and mapping improvements
(n) Propose land information systems and digitalization
(o) Address governor control over land transactions
(p) Review compensation frameworks for land acquisition
(q) Propose women's land rights protections
(r) Review community land rights and indigenous peoples
(s) Recommend investment climate improvements through secure land rights
(t) Advance land reform through comprehensive policy recommendations addressing Land Use Act and land administration systems

(j) Promote fiscal federalism principles
(k) Address revenue allocation disputes
(l) Balance federal, state and LGA revenue needs
(m) Consider population in allocation formula

(o) Promote horizontal equity among states
