## 721. NIGERIA INTEGRATED WATER RESOURCES MANAGEMENT COMMISSION

**MDA Code:** 252050001

Coordinates River Basin Development Authorities on water management. Develops national water resources policies and strategies. Addresses water allocation among agriculture, industry, domestic and environmental uses. Promotes sustainable water resources management. Coordinates stakeholders - government, communities, private sector. Addresses transboundary water issues with neighboring countries. Monitors water resources availability and allocation. Promotes water conservation and efficiency. Addresses climate change impacts on water resources. Collaborates with state water agencies. Faces challenges from competing water demands, climate variability, inadequate coordination and limited enforcement. Nigeria's water resources face increasing pressure from population growth, irrigation expansion and industrial development. Critical for water security, equitable allocation and sustainable water management balancing competing demands.

(h) Address transboundary water management with Benin, Niger, Chad, Cameroon
(i) Monitor water resources availability and allocation
(j) Promote water conservation and efficiency measures
(k) Address climate change impacts on water availability

(m) Balance upstream and downstream water users' needs

(q) Develop water pricing and cost recovery frameworks

(t) Advance water security and sustainable management balancing competing demands ensuring equitable allocation despite population pressure and climate challenges
