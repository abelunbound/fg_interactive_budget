## 761. NIGERIAN NAVY (NN)

**MDA Code:** 116004001

Nigerian Navy is maritime military service protecting territorial waters, economic zones and providing naval defense. Operating under Federal Ministry of Defence. Established to defend Nigeria's maritime domain, protect offshore assets and secure sea lines of communication. Defends Nigerian territorial waters and Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ). Protects offshore oil installations and platforms from attacks. Combats oil theft, illegal bunkering and pipeline vandalism at sea. Addresses piracy and maritime criminality in Gulf of Guinea. Provides maritime security and coastal patrol. Operates naval bases including Apapa, Calabar, Port Harcourt, Warri. Conducts anti-piracy operations in territorial waters. Collaborates with multinational maritime forces. Supports fisheries protection preventing illegal fishing. Conducts search and rescue operations at sea. Operates naval ships, patrol boats and aviation assets. Trains naval personnel and marine commandos. Faces challenges from aging fleet, limited modern platforms, oil theft costing billions and Gulf of Guinea piracy (once world's worst). Recent ship acquisitions improving capacity. Critical for maritime security, oil infrastructure protection and securing Nigeria's vast coastal waters despite fleet limitations.

**Core Mandate:** To defend maritime territory, protect offshore oil assets, combat piracy and oil theft, ensure maritime security and provide naval power for coastal defense.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Defend Nigerian territorial waters and maritime boundaries
(b) Protect Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) and continental shelf
(c) Guard offshore oil installations and platforms from attacks
(d) Combat oil theft, illegal bunkering and pipeline vandalism at sea
(e) Address piracy in Gulf of Guinea waters
(f) Conduct anti-piracy operations protecting shipping
(g) Provide maritime security and coastal patrol
(h) Operate naval bases (Apapa, Calabar, Port Harcourt, Warri)
(i) Collaborate with multinational maritime task forces
(j) Support fisheries protection preventing illegal fishing
(k) Conduct search and rescue operations at sea
(l) Operate naval ships, frigates and patrol boats
(m) Deploy Special Boat Service (SBS) commandos
(n) Provide riverine patrol in Niger Delta creeks
(o) Train naval personnel and officers
(p) Address aging naval fleet and equipment
(q) Combat oil theft costing Nigeria billions annually
(r) Address Gulf of Guinea piracy (formerly world's worst)
(s) Acquire modern naval platforms and vessels
(t) Advance maritime security protecting offshore oil infrastructure combating piracy and theft despite fleet limitations and Gulf of Guinea piracy challenges

## 763. NIGERIAN SAFETY INVESTIGATION BUREAU (NSIB)

Operates independently from regulatory agencies (NCAA, NIMASA). Publishes accident investigation reports. Promotes transportation safety culture. Collaborates with international investigation agencies (NTSB, AAIB). Implements ICAO Annex 13 standards for aircraft investigations. Addresses challenges from limited investigation capacity, equipment needs and implementation of safety recommendations. Aviation accidents require thorough investigation. Dana Air crash (2012), Associated Airlines crash (2013) highlighted importance. Independence from regulators critical for objective investigations. Critical for transportation safety, accident prevention and ensuring thorough independent investigations despite capacity constraints.

**Statutory Functions:**

(c) Investigate railway accidents and derailments
(d) Determine accident causes and contributing factors
(e) Analyze human factors, mechanical failures and systemic issues
(f) Issue safety recommendations to prevent accident recurrence
(g) Operate independently from regulatory agencies (NCAA, NIMASA, NRC)
(h) Publish comprehensive accident investigation reports
(i) Promote transportation safety culture and awareness
(j) Collaborate with NTSB (US), AAIB (UK) and international investigators
(k) Implement ICAO Annex 13 standards for investigations
(l) Recover flight data recorders (black boxes) from crash sites
(m) Conduct wreckage examination and analysis
(n) Interview witnesses and aviation personnel
(o) Make recommendations to aviation authorities
(p) Address limited investigation capacity and resources
(q) Acquire accident investigation equipment and tools
(r) Promote implementation of safety recommendations
(s) Monitor safety improvements from investigations
(t) Advance transportation safety through independent thorough investigations preventing future accidents despite capacity constraints
