## 226. FEDERAL PRODUCE INSPECTION

**MDA Code:** 222011001

Federal Produce Inspection is a specialized agency under Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment (formerly under Federal Ministry of Agriculture) responsible for inspection, grading, standardization and quality control of agricultural produce destined for export and domestic markets. The agency ensures Nigerian agricultural exports meet international quality standards and phytosanitary requirements. Operating produce inspection stations at ports, borders and major agricultural markets, the agency issues quality certificates for exports and enforces produce standards protecting Nigeria's reputation in international trade.

**Core Mandate:** To inspect, grade, standardize and certify quality of agricultural produce for export and domestic markets, ensuring compliance with national and international standards.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Inspect agricultural produce including cocoa, groundnut, palm produce, cotton, rubber and other export commodities
(b) Grade agricultural commodities according to national and international standards
(c) Issue quality certificates for agricultural exports meeting required standards
(d) Operate produce inspection stations at seaports, airports and land borders
(e) Enforce minimum quality standards for exported agricultural produce
(f) Conduct sampling and laboratory analysis of agricultural commodities
(g) Verify compliance with phytosanitary requirements for plant exports
(h) Inspect storage facilities and warehouses for produce quality maintenance
(i) Monitor produce handling, packaging and transportation standards
(j) Prevent export of substandard or adulterated agricultural products
(k) Collaborate with Standards Organisation of Nigeria on produce standards
(l) Coordinate with Nigerian Customs Service on export clearances
(m) Maintain produce inspection laboratories for quality testing
(n) Train produce inspectors and quality control personnel
(o) Develop and update produce grading standards
(p) Conduct market surveillance for produce quality in domestic markets
(q) Investigate cases of produce adulteration and contamination
(r) Promote good agricultural practices and post-harvest handling
(s) Issue export permits for agricultural commodities meeting standards
(t) Protect Nigeria's reputation in international agricultural commodity markets

## 233. FEDERAL ROAD MAINTENANCE AGENCY

(j) Operate road maintenance depots in all states

## 234. FEDERAL ROAD SAFETY COMMISSION

**MDA Code:** 161007001

The Federal Road Safety Commission (FRSC), popularly known as Federal Road Safety Corps, was established by Decree No. 45 of 1988 (now Federal Road Safety Commission (Establishment) Act 2007) to reduce road traffic crashes and create safe motoring environment. FRSC is leading government agency on road safety with mandate covering road traffic administration, safety campaigns, driver licensing, vehicle registration, highway patrol and accident response. Operating under Federal Ministry of Transport (previously Interior), FRSC maintains nationwide presence with commands in all states, sector commands, unit commands and outposts. The Corps employs Road Safety Marshals in regular and special categories.

**Core Mandate:** To reduce road traffic crashes, promote road safety awareness and create safe motoring environment across Nigeria.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Prevent and minimize road traffic crashes through enforcement and education
(b) Clear obstruction on highways for free flow of traffic
(c) Educate motorists and public on highway use importance
(d) Conduct research into road traffic accidents causes and prevention
(e) Determine speed limits for all categories of vehicles
(f) Conduct vehicle roadworthiness tests and issue certificates
(g) Issue driver's licenses through computerized system
(h) Design and produce number plates for vehicle registration
(i) Conduct highway patrol and traffic law enforcement
(j) Operate mobile courts for summary trial of traffic offenders
(k) Provide emergency rescue services at accident scenes
(l) Administer first aid and evacuation for accident victims
(m) Coordinate with Federal Fire Service on accident response
(n) Conduct road safety campaigns and public enlightenment
(o) Organize safety programmes for schools and communities
(p) Enforce use of seatbelts, helmets and child restraints
(q) Combat drunk driving and dangerous driving
(r) Regulate vehicle loading and overloading on highways
(s) Maintain road traffic data and accident statistics
(t) Train road safety personnel through FRSC training institutions
