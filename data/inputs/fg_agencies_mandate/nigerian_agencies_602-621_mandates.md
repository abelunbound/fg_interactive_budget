## 602. NATIONAL CENTRE FOR AGRICULTURAL MECHANISATION (NCAM), ILORIN

**MDA Code:** 215004001

National Centre for Agricultural Mechanisation is federal agricultural research institution developing agricultural machinery, mechanization technologies and farm equipment. Located in Ilorin, Kwara State in north-central Nigeria, operating under Agricultural Research Council of Nigeria and Federal Ministry of Agriculture. Established to promote agricultural mechanization reducing manual labor and increasing productivity. Develops tractors, implements and farm machinery adapted to Nigerian conditions. Researches appropriate mechanization technologies for smallholder farmers. Designs and fabricates tillage equipment, planters and harvesters. Develops processing equipment for cassava, rice, maize and other crops. Researches mechanization for different agroecological zones. Provides training on operation and maintenance of farm machinery. Fabricates prototypes of agricultural equipment. Conducts demonstrations showcasing mechanization benefits. Collaborates with tractor assembly plants and equipment manufacturers. Addresses challenges from high equipment costs, limited adoption by smallholders and maintenance difficulties. Nigeria's low mechanization (0.27 HP/ha vs. recommended 1.5 HP/ha) constrains productivity. Critical for agricultural transformation, reducing drudgery and increasing farm productivity.

**Core Mandate:** To develop agricultural machinery, promote mechanization technologies, train farmers and technicians, and advance agricultural mechanization reducing manual labor and increasing productivity.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Develop agricultural machinery and farm equipment for Nigerian agriculture
(b) Research appropriate mechanization technologies for smallholder farmers
(c) Design and fabricate tractors adapted to tropical conditions
(d) Develop tillage equipment including ploughs, harrows and cultivators
(e) Design planters, seeders and crop establishment equipment
(f) Develop harvesters and harvesting technologies
(g) Design processing equipment for cassava, rice and maize
(h) Develop threshing and winnowing machines
(i) Research mechanization for different agroecological zones
(j) Adapt mechanization to small farm sizes
(k) Develop power tillers for small-scale farmers
(l) Design irrigation pumps and water management equipment
(m) Provide training on operation of farm machinery
(n) Train technicians on maintenance and repair of equipment
(o) Fabricate prototypes and pilot equipment
(p) Conduct field demonstrations showcasing mechanization
(q) Collaborate with tractor manufacturers and equipment importers
(r) Promote affordable mechanization technologies
(s) Address Nigeria's low mechanization level (0.27 HP/ha)
(t) Advance agricultural mechanization and farm productivity reducing manual drudgery, increasing efficiency and supporting agricultural transformation

## 603. NATIONAL CENTRE FOR THE CONTROL OF SMALL ARMS AND LIGHT WEAPONS (NCCSALW)

Addresses challenges from porous borders, armed groups and limited enforcement capacity.
