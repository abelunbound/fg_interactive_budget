# NIGERIAN FEDERAL AGENCY MANDATES: AGENCIES 181-200

## 181. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF SOLID MINERALS DEVELOPMENT - HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 257001001

The Federal Ministry of Solid Minerals Development (formerly Ministry of Mines and Steel Development, renamed to emphasize solid minerals sector) was established in 1985 to unlock economic potentials of Nigeria's solid minerals sub-sector. The Ministry operates under Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act 2007 which repealed Minerals and Mining Act 1999, vesting all property in and control of mineral resources in Federal Government. Led by Minister of Solid Minerals Development (current: Dr. Oladele Henry Alake), the Ministry implements National Minerals and Metals Policy and Roadmap for Growth and Development of Nigerian Mining Industry (2016). The Ministry manages Mining Cadastre Office (MCO) as independent agency for mineral titles administration.

**Core Mandate:** To exploit Nigeria's mineral endowments in environmentally sustainable manner and establish vibrant minerals and metals industry for wealth creation, poverty reduction, economic growth and GDP contribution.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate and implement national solid minerals development policies and strategies
(b) Administer Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act 2007 and Minerals and Mining Regulations 2011
(c) Issue mineral titles including reconnaissance permits, exploration licences, small-scale mining leases, mining leases and quarry leases through Mining Cadastre Office
(d) Collect rents, fees and royalties from solid minerals exploitation
(e) Maintain Mining Cadastre cadastral maps and records of mineral title locations and ownership
(f) Conduct geological surveys through Nigerian Geological Survey Agency identifying mineral deposits
(g) Operate Mines Inspectorate Department inspecting mining operations for safety and standards compliance
(h) Enforce environmental compliance through Mines Environmental Compliance Department
(i) Regulate artisanal and small-scale mining operations
(j) Promote investment in mining sector and attract foreign direct investment
(k) Manage Solid Minerals Development Fund for sector financing
(l) Develop mining infrastructure and mineral processing facilities
(m) Regulate exploitation of gold, coal, bitumen, limestone, iron ore, lead-zinc, barites and other minerals
(n) Ensure sustainable mining practices and environmental protection
(o) Coordinate Federal Mines Offices in all 36 states and FCT
(p) Promote local content and value addition in mining sector
(q) Combat illegal mining and mineral smuggling
(r) Facilitate technology transfer and mining skills development
(s) Maintain mineral title database and geo-mining information systems
(t) Ensure mining sector contribution to GDP and economic diversification

## 182. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF SPECIAL DUTIES & INTER-GOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS HQTRS

(g) Oversee National Senior Citizens Centre promoting welfare of elderly citizens
(h) Coordinate special projects cutting across multiple ministries
(i) Facilitate inter-governmental cooperation on national development priorities
(j) Handle liaison functions with state governments on federal programmes
(k) Coordinate special economic empowerment programmes
(l) Facilitate resolution of boundary disputes between states and local governments
(m) Coordinate national population and housing census in collaboration with National Population Commission
(n) Handle protocol and ceremonial functions for special government events
(o) Facilitate inter-governmental forums and Governor's Forum engagements
(p) Coordinate federal government interventions in states during emergencies

## 185. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF WATER RESOURCES AND SANITATION - HQTRS

Following cabinet reshuffle, sanitation functions previously under environment or health were consolidated with water resources recognizing linkage between water supply and sanitation (WASH sector).

**Statutory Functions:**

(c) Coordinate 14 River Basin Development Authorities for river basin management
(d) Promote irrigation development for agricultural productivity
(e) Implement National Water Supply and Sanitation Policy
(f) Coordinate rural water supply and sanitation programmes
(g) Develop urban water supply infrastructure through water boards
(h) Manage flood control measures and flood plain management
(i) Coordinate hydrological services and water resources data collection
(j) Regulate water resources allocation and water rights
(k) Promote integrated water resources management (IWRM)
(l) Coordinate transboundary water resources management (Lake Chad, Niger Basin)
(m) Develop groundwater resources and boreholes for water supply
(n) Promote water treatment and water quality monitoring
(o) Coordinate sanitation infrastructure including sewerage systems and waste water treatment
(p) Implement Open Defecation Free (ODF) campaigns and sanitation behavior change
(q) Promote hygiene education and community-led total sanitation (CLTS)
(r) Coordinate WASH (Water, Sanitation and Hygiene) sector development
(s) Facilitate public-private partnerships in water supply and sanitation
(t) Ensure water security and climate change adaptation for water resources
