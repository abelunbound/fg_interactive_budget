# NIGERIAN FEDERAL AGENCY MANDATES: AGENCIES 801-820

## 801. PORT HEALTH SERVICES (PHS), ABUJA

**MDA Code:** 521029001

Port Health Services Abuja operates at Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Federal Capital Territory. Located at Nigeria's second-busiest airport serving capital city. Screens international arrivals, conducts health inspections and enforces quarantine regulations. Capital city entry point handles high diplomatic and business traveler traffic. COVID-19 testing center for international travelers. Verifies yellow fever vaccination certificates. Monitors malaria and infectious disease surveillance at FCT gateway.

**Core Mandate:** To prevent disease importation through Abuja airport, screen international travelers, enforce health regulations and protect FCT from imported infectious diseases.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Screen international travelers arriving at Nnamdi Azikiwe Airport
(b) Verify yellow fever vaccination certificates for entry compliance
(c) Conduct COVID-19 testing and vaccination verification
(d) Enforce International Health Regulations (IHR 2005) at airport
(e) Quarantine suspected infectious disease cases
(f) Issue health certificates for international travel
(g) Monitor disease surveillance at FCT entry point
(h) Inspect aircraft for health hazards and sanitation
(i) Screen airline crew for infectious diseases
(j) Conduct temperature screening for travelers
(k) Test for infectious diseases as required
(l) Monitor WHO disease alerts affecting travelers
(m) Collaborate with NCDC on outbreak response
(n) Coordinate with Immigration on traveler screening
(o) Maintain health screening equipment and facilities
(p) Record and report disease surveillance data
(q) Enforce quarantine for travelers from outbreak areas
(r) Provide public health information to travelers
(s) Inspect food and water on aircraft
(t) Protect Abuja and Nigeria from imported diseases through airport health screening

## 816. NIGERIA POLICE FORCE - FORMATIONS AND COMMANDS

**MDA Code:** 155004001

Nigeria Police Force operates nationwide through State Commands in all 36 states and FCT, 12 Zonal Commands, Area Commands, Divisions and Stations. Specialized units include Mobile Police (MOPOL), Counter-Terrorism Unit, Explosive Ordnance Disposal, Criminal Investigation Department (CID) and Special Protection Unit. SARS (Special Anti-Robbery Squad) dissolved 2020 following #EndSARS protests. Approximately 370,000 officers serve population exceeding 200 million.

**Core Mandate:** To enforce laws, prevent and detect crime, maintain public order, protect lives and property, conduct investigations and ensure internal security nationwide.

(f) Protect lives and property of citizens

(h) Provide community policing and public safety
(i) Combat armed robbery, kidnapping and violent crimes
(j) Address banditry in northwestern Nigeria
(k) Investigate cybercrime and fraud
(l) Provide traffic management and road safety
(m) Conduct criminal intelligence gathering
(n) Operate Mobile Police for rapid deployment
(o) Respond to emergency calls and distress
(p) Conduct crime scene investigations
(q) Maintain criminal records and databases
(r) Collaborate with other security agencies
(s) Provide court security and witness protection
(t) Advance law enforcement and public safety through nationwide police operations

## 817. POLICE SERVICE COMMISSION (PSC)

**MDA Code:** 160001001

Police Service Commission is independent constitutional federal body responsible for Nigeria Police Force personnel management. Operates under 1999 Constitution Third Schedule Part I. Recruits, appoints, promotes and disciplines police officers from Constable to Inspector-General rank. Ensures merit-based professional police force independent of political interference.

**Core Mandate:** To recruit, promote and discipline police officers, ensure police professionalism, maintain police independence and support effective law enforcement through merit-based management.

**Statutory Functions:**

(b) Appoint police officers from Constable to Inspector-General
(c) Promote police officers based on performance and seniority
(d) Discipline police officers for misconduct and offenses
(e) Dismiss police officers for serious violations
(f) Ensure professional standards in Nigeria Police Force
(g) Maintain police independence from political interference
(h) Operate as constitutional body under Third Schedule
(i) Investigate serious police misconduct
(j) Conduct competency assessments for promotions
(k) Ensure transparent recruitment processes
(l) Promote competent officers to leadership positions
(m) Address police corruption through disciplinary measures
(n) Ensure gender balance in police recruitment
(o) Implement diversity in police appointments
(p) Collaborate with Police Force on personnel management
(q) Review police personnel policies
