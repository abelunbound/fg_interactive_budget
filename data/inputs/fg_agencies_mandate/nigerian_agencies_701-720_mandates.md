## 703. NIGER DELTA DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION (NDDC)

NDDC represents Niger Delta development hopes vs. corruption reality. Critical for regional development, peace and addressing oil-producing region's marginalization despite enormous challenges.

(h) Support skills acquisition and vocational training
(i) Empower youth reducing militancy and restiveness
(j) Develop water supply and sanitation infrastructure
(k) Address coastal erosion devastating Delta communities
(l) Build jetties and riverine transportation infrastructure
(m) Support agriculture and fisheries development
(n) Receive percentage of oil revenues for development
