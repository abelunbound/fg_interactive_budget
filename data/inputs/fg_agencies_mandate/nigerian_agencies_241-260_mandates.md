## 241. FEDERAL SCHOOL OF SURVEY, OYO

**MDA Code:** 253017001

The Federal School of Survey, Oyo, Oyo State, is Nigeria's pioneer and only specialized institution for surveying and geoinformatics education. Established in 1908 as Government Departmental Training Institution attached to Colonial Survey Department in Lagos, the institution is older than Nigeria itself (named in 1914). The School moved from Lagos to Okene, then Ibadan (1926), finally settling in Oyo (1934). Became federal institution under Federal Ministry of Works and Housing in 1967. Located on 250 hectares in Oyo, the School awards National Diploma and Higher National Diploma in Surveying and Geoinformatics. As first modern surveying training institution in Nigeria and Africa, approximately 80-85% of Nigeria's registered professional surveyors are products of this institution. The School has trained over 27,500 surveyors since inception, serving Nigeria, Commonwealth countries and Sub-Saharan Africa. Currently under Federal Ministry of Works, there are proposals to upgrade to Federal University of Geomatics.

**Core Mandate:** To train professional land surveyors, geoinformatics specialists and survey technologists providing surveying manpower for national development and Africa.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Provide National Diploma in Surveying and Geoinformatics
(b) Award Higher National Diploma in Surveying and Geoinformatics
(c) Train land surveyors in cadastral surveying and land administration
(d) Provide training in engineering surveying for construction and infrastructure
(e) Offer training in geodetic surveying and geodesy
(f) Conduct training in hydrographic surveying for marine and coastal surveys
(g) Provide training in photogrammetry and remote sensing
(h) Offer training in Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and spatial data management
(i) Conduct training in cartography and map production
(j) Provide training in quantity surveying and valuation
(k) Offer training in mining surveying and underground measurements
(l) Conduct training in geoinformatics and spatial analysis
(m) Provide training in Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS/GPS)
(n) Offer training in digital mapping and computer-aided design
(o) Conduct field training and practical surveying exercises
(p) Maintain surveying equipment including total stations, GPS receivers and levels
(q) Prepare graduates for registration with Surveyors Council of Nigeria (SURCON)
(r) Conduct applied research in surveying and geoinformatics
(s) Provide consultancy services on surveying and mapping projects
(t) Train surveyors for monitoring environmental disasters, area mapping for security, and development planning
