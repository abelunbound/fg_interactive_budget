NDA graduates form officer corps backbone. Critical for military leadership development, professional officer training and producing educated military commanders.

(g) Train Flying Officers for Nigerian Air Force
(h) Provide military science and leadership education
(i) Conduct physical training and military discipline
(j) Teach warfare tactics and military strategy
(k) Combine academic rigor with military standards
