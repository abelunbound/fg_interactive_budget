## 81. Federal College of Agriculture, Ishiagu
**MDA Code:** 215023001

The Federal College of Agriculture, Ishiagu (FCAI) is a Federal Government-owned agricultural training institution located in Ishiagu, Ivo Local Government Area of Ebonyi State. The institution traces its origins to the School of Agriculture established in 1955 at Umudike by the Eastern Nigeria Government. In 1964, it was amalgamated with the Agricultural Research and Training Station, Umudike. The institution assumed federal status as the Federal Agricultural Research and Training Station in 1972, becoming the training wing of the National Root Crops Research Institute in 1976.

Following accreditation by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE) in 1989, the institution became the Federal College of Agriculture, Umudike. Administrative autonomy was granted by the Federal Government in 1991, and the college was relocated to Ishiagu in 1993 following the establishment of a Federal University on its Umudike campus.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college has the national mandate to develop and organise high-quality, practically oriented training with dynamic curricula to produce self-reliant technicians and technologists at National Diploma and Higher National Diploma levels. Its functions include promoting agricultural training, production, and research in Nigeria; providing extension education and services; integrating system-wide innovations to achieve tangible results; pursuing sustainable capacity building, consultancy, and innovative outreach; increasing agricultural productivity for food security; reducing poverty and unemployment; and securing livelihoods through sustainable agriculture and human development to meet national and global challenges. The college collaborates with the National Root Crops Research Institute, Umudike, for research endeavours and offers NBTE-accredited programmes preparing graduates eligible for NYSC participation.

## 82. Federal College of Agriculture, Moore Plantation, Ibadan
**MDA Code:** 215022001

The Federal College of Agriculture, Ibadan (formerly School of Agriculture) is Nigeria's premier agricultural training institution, established in 1921 by Mr. O.T. Faulkner, the then Director of Agriculture. The college is located at Moore Plantation, Apata, Ibadan, Oyo State, one of the oldest agricultural stations in Nigeria established in 1899 as a model farm for propagating rubber trees and general agricultural improvement. The station became an experimental facility in 1905 for cotton research directed by British High Commissioner Sir Ralph Moor, after whom it was named.

The institution is supervised by the Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development through the Agricultural Research Council of Nigeria established by Act 44 of May 26, 1999.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The mandate is to produce diplomats at both National Diploma and Higher National Diploma levels with high academic standards and sound practical skills to meet the demands of the agricultural sector. The college aims to contribute towards achieving Nigeria's goals in agriculture through qualitative education and production of well-equipped middle-level manpower. Core functions include providing qualitative agricultural education including vocational and entrepreneurial training; enhancing agricultural production; serving as a hub for middle-level manpower development in agriculture; providing hands-on training to practically equip students for agricultural processes from input to production and value addition towards food security. Academic programmes span Agricultural Extension and Management, Agricultural Environment and Bio-Engineering, Home and Rural Economics, Horticulture, Animal Production Technology, and related fields.

## 83. Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology, Ibadan
**MDA Code:** 265004001

The Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology, Ibadan is located at Moore Plantation, Apata, Ibadan, Oyo State. It operates as a parastatal under the Federal Ministry of Livestock Development. The institution was established to address the shortage of trained personnel for livestock disease diagnosis and control in the former Western Region of Nigeria, particularly for poultry diseases.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college mandate is to produce well-trained and morally sound graduates to become self-employed in the areas of Science and Agriculture. It aims to produce graduates at National Diploma and Higher National Diploma levels to meet the needs of farmers, agro-allied industries, pharmaceutical companies, and government agencies. Core functions include: producing technicians capable of carrying out routine preventive measures and advisory services in livestock, fishery, and crop production; establishing linkages and collaboration with policy makers, research institutes, and training institutions; enabling graduates to establish and manage modern standard livestock farms, veterinary clinics, and diagnostic laboratories; promoting capacity building through outreach programmes, vocational training, and continuous education; and promoting youth development and empowerment opportunities through entrepreneurial and home economics activities. The college is the only Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology in the southern part of Nigeria.

## 84. Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology, Vom
**MDA Code:** 265005001

The Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology, Vom is located within the National Veterinary Research Institute (NVRI), Vom, Jos South Local Government Area, Plateau State. The college was established in 1941 as the Veterinary School, Vom, with an initial intake of ten students. It has the distinction of being the first higher institution in West Africa to produce graduate Veterinary Surgeons before the first university was established in Nigeria at Ibadan in 1948.

In March 1980, the Veterinary School was formally accorded college status and renamed College of Animal Health and Husbandry with a mandate to award Ordinary and Higher Diplomas. The National Science and Technology Amendment Decree No. 46 of December 30, 1990, gave legal backing to conduct training for technicians and technologists. The college was renamed Federal College of Animal Health and Production Technology on January 29, 1991.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college is mandated to train middle-level manpower in Animal Health, Animal Production, Livestock Extension/Management Services, and Vocational Training who can effectively utilise their training to establish and manage livestock farms. Its guiding principles include: improving service to the livestock sector through effective training; training minds to appreciate the significance of animal resources in national development; inculcating the importance of practical self-commitment to the dignity of labour for national self-sufficiency in animal protein production; and producing extension staff who can bring agro-veterinary research and innovation to livestock farmers and feedback farmers' problems to appropriate authorities. Programmes include Pre-ND, ND and HND in Animal Health and Production Technology, HND in Animal Health, HND in Animal Production, HND in Agricultural Extension and Management, and Veterinary Science Laboratory Technology.

## 85. Federal College of Complementary and Alternative Medicine, Nigeria
**MDA Code:** 521027011

The Federal College of Complementary and Alternative Medicine (FEDCAM) was established by the Federal Government of Nigeria as a parastatal of the Federal Ministry of Health based on recommendations from the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN). The Federal College of Complementary and Alternative Medicine (Establishment) Act 2021 was passed by the National Assembly as an unconventional institution for the pursuit of academic and professional knowledge in complementary and alternative medicine.

The institution's academic activities were suspended by the National Universities Commission (NUC) in October 2010 for running degree programmes without mandatory approvals. In 2011, the Federal Ministry of Health suspended the management and instituted an Interim Administrative Team. There are ongoing plans to revive FEDCAM as part of the Federal Government's initiative to integrate traditional medicine into the national healthcare system.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
FEDCAM was established with the mandate to train alternative medicine practitioners in healthcare delivery services; carry out clinical works; provide alternative methods of treating ailments; and conduct research in complementary and alternative medicine. Its functions include training and retraining practitioners of Complementary and Alternative Medicine in Nigeria; researching and developing all aspects of Complementary and Alternative Medicine; contributing to formulating regulatory standards; and supporting integration of complementary and alternative medicine into the mainstream Nigerian healthcare delivery system. The college covers modalities including Homeopathy, Naturopathy, Acupuncture, Osteopathy, and Chiropractic practices.

## 86. Federal College of Dental Technology and Therapy, Enugu
**MDA Code:** 521016001

The Federal College of Dental Technology and Therapy, Enugu is a federal tertiary institution located in Trans-Ekulu, Enugu State. It was established in 1955 in Lagos as the Federal School of Dental Technology and Hygiene, training dental technicians and hygienists. In 1978, it was renamed the Federal School of Dental Technology and Therapy with the expanded goal of training dental technologists and therapists. The school relocated to its present location in Enugu in 1982.

The institution was re-established by Nigerian law via the Federal College of Dental Technology and Therapy (Establishment) Act No. 8 of 2017, which granted the power to award degrees. President Muhammadu Buhari signed the Act on December 5, 2017. In February 2021, the National Universities Commission approved the college to run undergraduate degree programmes in collaboration with Federal University of Technology, Owerri.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college is mandated to provide academic and professional training in dental technology and therapy to meet Nigeria's critical healthcare manpower needs. Under Section 6(a) of the Establishment Act, the college is empowered to award degrees, diplomas, and certificates for courses provided under the Act. Core functions include: producing dental technologists and dental therapists for various medical institutions; providing patient care and continuing education opportunities; contributing research discoveries to improve dental healthcare; offering National Diploma, Higher National Diploma, and Bachelor of Technology degree programmes in dental technology and therapy; and serving as a recognised Centre of Excellence in Africa for dental technology and therapy education. The school was once the only institution training dental technologists and therapists in Nigeria.

## 87. Federal College of Education (Technical), Keana

Federal College of Education (Technical), Keana is a newly established federal institution located in Keana Local Government Area, Nasarawa State. The Act specifically establishing the college was assented to on February 8, 2023, and gazetted in Government Notice 7, No. 29 Vol. 110 of February 13, 2023. Core functions include: training qualified technical and vocational teachers for Nigeria's basic education system and technological development; producing educators with intellectual and professional background adequate for their teaching assignment; fostering adaptability to changing educational and national situations; preparing graduates for contributions to the world of work; conducting research in technical education; and organising workshops, seminars, and in-service courses to update practicing teachers' knowledge.

## 88. Federal College of Education (Technical), Yauri

Core functions include: offering full-time courses in teaching, instruction, and training across disciplines including technology, applied science, arts, social sciences, humanities, and management; producing transformative leaders and innovators who drive sustainable development and technological advancement; training skilled educators and technicians for Nigeria's industrial and economic growth; and equipping students with practical, industry-relevant skills for the job market.

## 89. Federal College of Education, Abeokuta
**MDA Code:** 517019001

Federal College of Education, Abeokuta was established in 1976 as the Federal Advanced Teachers College, one of four similar colleges alongside those at Okene, Pankshin, and Yola. It is the first tertiary institution in Ogun State. The college started at the then Abeokuta Grammar School, Isale-Igbein, in the centre of Abeokuta, the capital of Ogun State. The foundation day is January 27, 1977, when the first set of 86 students were registered.

The college moved to its permanent site in 1983 at Osiele along the Abeokuta-Ibadan Road, occupying a land area of 632.95 hectares vested by the Federal Government via Gazette No. 4164 of August 25, 1977. In 2010, the Centre for Degree Programmes (CEDEP) was established following resource assessment by the University of Ibadan and National Universities Commission.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college's primary mandate is to provide full-time courses of teaching, instruction, and training in technology, applied science, commerce, arts, social sciences, humanities, and management; and to carry out research in the development and adaptation of techniques. Core functions include: offering three-year full-time and five-year sandwich courses leading to the Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE); producing highly motivated and skilled teachers for Primary, Junior Secondary, and Senior Secondary Schools; providing degree programmes in affiliation with the University of Ibadan and Lagos State University; offering Post Graduate Diploma in Education (PGDE); and providing an all-round total quality teaching, research, and public service environment.

## 90. Federal College of Education (Technical), Akoka
**MDA Code:** 517019002

The Federal College of Education (Technical), Akoka (formerly National Technical Teachers College) was established in 1967 by the Federal Government of Nigeria following the Gailers' Report of August 1964 titled "A National Plan for the Development of Technical Education in the Federal Republic of Nigeria." The institution was the first Technical Teacher Education institution in the country, initially awarding Technical Teachers' Certificate (TTC) for professionals in commercial and technical education.

In 1982, the college was renamed Federal College of Education (Technical), Akoka, due to expansion in curricula and courses. The college became a fully autonomous academic institution in 1986 with the promulgation of the Federal College of Education Decree No. 4 of 1986 (as amended in 1993). The college moved to its permanent site along St. Finbarr's College Road, Akoka, in September 1975. It is approved by the National Universities Commission and affiliated with Federal University of Technology, Minna, and University of Benin for degree programmes.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college mandate is grooming and producing teachers in technical, vocational, and commercial academic disciplines. Core functions include: producing self-reliant Technical and Vocational Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE) graduates grounded in Information and Communication Technology, research, and teaching; preparing students to instruct technical subjects at elementary, secondary, and professional levels; offering NCE programmes in Technical Education, Vocational Education, Science Education, and Business Education; awarding undergraduate degrees through university affiliations; and contributing to the technological and socio-economic development of Nigeria through effective research for sustainable living.

## 91. Federal College of Education (Technical), Asaba
**MDA Code:** 517019003

Federal College of Education (Technical), Asaba is a federal government higher education institution located in Asaba City, Delta State. Decree No. 4, published in the Federal Republic of Nigeria Extraordinary Official Gazette No. 16 Vol. 73 of March 21, 1986, gave birth to the institution. The college began operations in 1987 at the former Asaba Technical College (ATC) as its temporary site. Asaba is a rapidly developing urban centre and administrative headquarters of Delta State.

The college has relocated to its permanent site along Asaba/Ibusa Road. It is affiliated with the University of Benin for degree programmes and has been approved for independent degree operations under the Federal Colleges of Education Act 2023.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The basic mission of the college is to improve the quality of Technical Education in Nigeria. Core functions include: training professional teachers in technical, vocational, and commercial academic areas; offering pre-degree, NCE, Professional Diploma in Education (PDE), and degree programmes; running competitive integrated foundation year programmes for students who do not meet standard entry criteria; providing world-class learning facilities with well-equipped laboratories, modern equipment, and innovative technologies; preparing students for the education sector; and fostering excellence in learning through multi-media classrooms and modern libraries. The college comprises the School of Education, School of Business Education, School of Technical Education, School of Vocational Education, and School of Computer and Science Education.

## 93. Federal College of Education, Eha-Amufu
**MDA Code:** 517019020

Federal College of Education, Eha-Amufu is a federal government higher education institution located in Eha-Amufu, Enugu State. The college was established on February 21, 1981, by Chief Jim Ifeanyichukwu Nwobodo, the then Governor of old Anambra State. The Anambra State House of Assembly enacted Anambra State of Nigeria Law No. 28 of 1982, retroactively establishing the college effective from February 21, 1981.

The site was formerly occupied by the Federal Government-owned Teacher Training College (TTC), Eha-Amufu. Following state creation in 1991, Enugu State Government inherited the college. The Federal Government took over in April 1993 with financial effect from January 1994. Decree No. 4 of 1986, amended by Decree No. 34 of 1993 and Decree No. 6 of 1993, established the Federal College of Education, Eha-Amufu as the 20th Federal College of Education. It is affiliated with the University of Nigeria for degree programmes.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college mission is to be a distinguished centre of academic excellence and innovation for pre-service and in-service teacher education as well as efficient and effective management. Core functions include: training students who make significant impact in Nigeria's educational sector; providing academic excellence, vocational education, and high moral training; offering NCE, Degree, and Professional Diploma in Education programmes; establishing a Directorate of Entrepreneurship to equip students with skills for self-reliance and job creation; and conducting research in teacher education. The college has received accreditation from NCCE for 17 academic programmes.

## 94. Federal College of Education (Technical), Ekiadolor

## 97. Federal College of Education (Technical), Gusau
**MDA Code:** 517019006

Federal College of Education (Technical), Gusau is a federal-owned conventional degree and NCE awarding institution located in Gusau, the capital of Zamfara State. The college was officially established in 1989 under the Federal College of Education Decree 1989 (as amended in 1993), and academic activities commenced in May 1990 with an initial intake of 110 students in the School of Business Education and School of Industrial Education.

The college has a unique distinction as the only institution in Africa specifically established for training women technical teachers. It was founded to encourage women to take up technical and vocational subjects for the Nigeria Certificate in Education. The college has graduated over 5,000 women NCE graduates since establishment, with current female-only student enrolment exceeding 7,000. It is affiliated with Federal University Gusau for degree programmes.

**Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college mandate is to train and graduate competent female technology teachers and potential entrepreneurs for technological and national development. Core functions include: producing specialist teachers in technical, vocational, business, and science subjects for the education system; encouraging women to participate in technical and vocational fields; offering NCE programmes in Electrical/Electronics Technology, Computer Science, Fine and Applied Arts, Home Economics, Physical and Health Education, and other technical subjects; providing professional diploma programmes; and supporting the training of female technical educators for Nigeria's development needs. The college offers both NCE and degree programmes under its dual mandate.

## 100. Federal College of Education, Ilawe-Ekiti
**MDA Code:** 517019033

Federal College of Education, Ilawe-Ekiti, Ekiti State was established by the Federal Government of Nigeria in April 2023. In May 2023, President Muhammadu Buhari approved the establishment, and President Bola Tinubu approved the immediate take-off. The temporary site is the erstwhile Government Girls College, Ilawe-Ekiti, along Ilawe-Ikere Road, covering approximately 4 hectares.

The permanent site, also along Ilawe-Ikere Road, covers 107.813 hectares of land donated by the Ilawe community. **Core Mandate and Functions:**
The college mandate is to promote the production of qualified teachers at the foundational and basic segment of the education sector. It is designed as a two-in-one institution serving as both a College of Education and University, awarding both NCE Certificate and Degrees. Core functions include: running courses in Education, Sciences, and Technology; extending programmes to vocational and allied studies to produce job creators; equipping future educators with knowledge, skills, and professional values for effective teaching; offering NCE programmes across six schools: School of Arts and Social Sciences, School of Early Childhood Care and Primary Education, School of General Education, School of Languages, School of Science, and School of Vocational and Technical Education; and demonstrating the Federal Government's determination to expand educational opportunities and invest in training future educators.
