## 661. NATIONAL OPEN UNIVERSITY OF NIGERIA (NOUN)

**MDA Code:** 517024001

National Open University of Nigeria is federal open and distance learning university providing flexible tertiary education. Headquartered in Abuja with study centers nationwide, operating under Federal Ministry of Education. Established in 1983, closed in 1984, re-established in 2002 as Nigeria's only single-mode open and distance learning (ODL) university. Provides undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs through distance learning. Admits working adults, professionals and non-traditional students. Offers flexible learning accommodating work schedules. Provides programs in arts, sciences, education, management sciences, law, agriculture and engineering. Operates study centers across Nigeria for student support. Delivers courses through print materials, online platforms and multimedia. Conducts virtual lectures and e-learning. Operates facilitation centers for practical sessions and examinations. Admits over 500,000 students making it Africa's largest single-mode ODL university. Provides affordable tertiary education for those unable to attend conventional universities. Promotes lifelong learning and continuing education. Addresses access barriers - working adults, married women, rural residents. Faces challenges from quality concerns, limited practical training, inadequate ICT infrastructure and student support needs. Critical for expanding tertiary education access, democratizing university education and providing flexible learning pathways.

**Core Mandate:** To provide open and distance learning university education, expand tertiary access through flexible programs, serve working adults and non-traditional students, and democratize higher education.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Provide open and distance learning university education for Nigerians
(b) Admit working adults, professionals and non-traditional students
(c) Offer flexible degree programs accommodating work schedules
(d) Provide undergraduate programs in arts, sciences and social sciences
(e) Offer postgraduate programs (Masters and PhD) through distance learning
(f) Deliver programs in education, management, law, agriculture and engineering
(g) Operate study centers nationwide for student support services
(h) Deliver courses through print materials, course guides and textbooks
(i) Provide online learning platforms and e-learning systems
(j) Conduct virtual lectures and video-based instruction
(k) Operate facilitation centers for practical sessions and examinations
(l) Admit 500,000+ students as Africa's largest single-mode ODL university
(m) Provide affordable tertiary education for disadvantaged groups
(n) Promote lifelong learning and continuing education
(o) Address access barriers for working adults and married women
(p) Serve rural residents unable to relocate to conventional universities
(q) Provide teacher professional development through distance programs
(r) Conduct research on open and distance learning pedagogy
(s) Collaborate with international open universities
(t) Advance tertiary education access democratizing university education through flexible distance learning serving 500,000+ students unable to attend conventional universities

## 671. NATIONAL PENSION COMMISSION (PenCom)

**MDA Code:** 161013001

National Pension Commission is federal regulatory agency overseeing Nigeria's Contributory Pension Scheme. Headquartered in Abuja, operating under Pension Reform Act 2014. Established to regulate pension industry, protect pension fund assets and ensure retirement income security. Regulates Contributory Pension Scheme for public and private sector workers. Licenses and supervises Pension Fund Administrators (PFAs) managing pension contributions. Regulates Pension Fund Custodians (PFCs) safeguarding pension assets. Enforces mandatory pension contributions (18% - 10% employer, 8% employee). Maintains database of pension contributors and Retirement Savings Accounts (RSAs). Approves pension fund investments ensuring safety and returns. Addresses employer non-remittance of pension deductions. Promotes pension coverage expansion. Regulates Micro Pension Plan for informal sector workers. Collaborates with NSITF on workplace pensions. Pension assets exceed N15 trillion managed by PFAs. Faces challenges from employer non-compliance (many employers deduct but don't remit), limited informal sector coverage and investment returns concerns. Pension reform transformed Nigeria from pay-as-you-go to funded system. Critical for retirement security, pension assets protection and ensuring workers have income in old age.

**Core Mandate:** To regulate Contributory Pension Scheme, supervise pension operators, protect pension assets, ensure compliance, expand coverage and secure workers' retirement income.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Regulate Contributory Pension Scheme for public and private sector
(b) License and supervise Pension Fund Administrators (PFAs)

(d) Enforce mandatory pension contributions (18% of salary)
(e) Maintain database of pension contributors nationwide
(f) Register Retirement Savings Accounts (RSAs) for workers
(g) Approve pension fund investment guidelines
(h) Monitor pension fund investments ensuring safety and returns
(i) Address employer non-remittance of pension deductions
(j) Sanction employers failing to remit pension contributions
(k) Promote pension coverage expansion
(l) Regulate Micro Pension Plan for informal sector workers
(m) Protect pension fund assets (N15+ trillion managed)
(n) Ensure pension payments to retirees
(o) Monitor PFA compliance with regulations
(p) Address contributor complaints and disputes
(q) Promote pension awareness and education
(r) Collaborate with NSITF on workplace pensions
(s) Monitor demographic trends affecting pension sustainability
(t) Advance retirement security regulating Contributory Pension Scheme protecting N15+ trillion pension assets ensuring workers' retirement income despite employer non-compliance challenges

## 672. NATIONAL POPULATION COMMISSION (NPC)

Accurate population data critical for planning but census delays create data vacuum. Critical for development planning, resource allocation and demographic information despite census implementation challenges.

(f) Maintain national vital registration database
(g) Conduct demographic surveys on fertility and mortality
(h) Provide population projections and estimates
(i) Conduct population research on demographic trends
(j) Monitor population growth and demographic changes
(k) Provide population data for development planning
(l) Support resource allocation using demographic information
(m) Conduct household surveys on demographic characteristics
(n) Monitor migration patterns and population movements
(o) Collaborate with UNFPA on population programs
(p) Address census challenges and political sensitivities
(q) Improve vital registration coverage (currently <40% births)
(r) Promote birth registration awareness
(s) Provide demographic data for health and education planning
(t) Advance development planning through population data addressing critical census delays (2006 last census) and demographic data vacuum requiring urgent census implementation
