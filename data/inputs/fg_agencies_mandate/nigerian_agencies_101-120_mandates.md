## 101. FEDERAL COLLEGE OF EDUCATION ISU, EBONYI STATE

**MDA Code:** 517019025

The Federal College of Education (Technical) Isu, Ebonyi State, is one of six Federal Colleges of Education established by the Federal Government in May 2020 under President Muhammadu Buhari's administration, distributed across Nigeria's six geo-political zones to address teacher shortages. Located in Isuokoma, Onicha Local Government Area of Ebonyi State, representing the South-East zone allocation, the college operates under the Federal Colleges of Education Act CAP F8 LFN 2004. The institution's core mandate is to provide full-time courses of teaching, instruction, and training in technology, applied science, commerce, arts, social sciences, humanities and management. Specific functions include: (a) to establish an academic institution where excellence and discipline in technology and learning produce professionally trained and skilled teachers with technical finesse; (b) to produce qualified teachers holding the Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE) capable of teaching in primary and junior secondary schools; (c) to establish and maintain such schools and other teaching units within the college as the Council may determine; (d) to institute and award fellowships, medals, prizes and other academic titles; (e) to mount exhibitions and displays designed to foster appreciation of trends in educational scope and requirements; (f) to carry out research in the development and adaptation of educational techniques; (g) to erect, provide, equip and maintain educational, recreational and residential facilities; and (h) to perform other acts that may advance the college's objectives. The college commenced academic operations in October 2021 under pioneer Provost Prof. Reuben Okechukwu.

## 110. FEDERAL COLLEGE OF EDUCATION OYO

**MDA Code:** 517019014

The Federal College of Education (Special), Oyo, was established on October 5, 1977, as Federal Advanced Teachers College (Special) and is the only specialized college of its kind in Nigeria and Sub-Saharan Africa dedicated to special education. Operating under Federal Colleges of Education Act CAP F8 LFN 2004, specifically listed in the First Schedule as "Federal College of Education (Special), Oyo Town, Oyo State," the college was upgraded from Federal Advanced Teachers College to Federal College of Education with mandate to award National Certificate of Education (NCE) in 1977 during General Olusegun Obasanjo's military administration. The college's unique mandate is to train teachers and para-professionals at the NCE level who, upon graduation, can handle both normal and special needs pupils/students in any classroom situation in primary and lower secondary education. Specific functions include: (a) to produce qualified teachers in special education holding the Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE) specializing in teaching students with various disabilities including visual impairment, hearing impairment, intellectual disabilities, physical disabilities, and learning difficulties; (b) to train para-professionals in special education fields; (c) to provide full-time courses of teaching, instruction and training in technology, applied science, commerce, arts, social sciences, humanities and management, with special education integration; (d) to carry out research in development and adaptation of special education techniques and inclusive education methodologies; (e) to establish and maintain specialized teaching units and facilities for students with disabilities; (f) to provide specialized facilities for teaching and training teachers of the handicapped; (g) to accommodate and educate persons with disabilities; (h) to mount exhibitions fostering appreciation of special education needs; and (i) to perform acts advancing special education objectives. According to UNDP/UNESCO 1996 report (NIR/87/008), the college has the best qualified staff in Special Education in Nigeria and West, North, East and Central Africa.

## 114. FEDERAL COLLEGE OF FISHERIES AND MARINE TECHNOLOGY - LAGOS

**MDA Code:** 254011001

The Federal College of Fisheries and Marine Technology, Lagos, assumed its present status as a full-fledged technological institution in 1992, having been established in 1969 as Federal School of Fisheries. Located at 2 Wilmot Point Road, Bar Beach, Victoria Island, Lagos, the college operates as a monotechnic approved by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE). The transformation from vocational training institute to composite College of Technology resulted from comprehensive re-examination of training scope necessitated by phenomenal growth of Nigeria's fisheries and maritime sector requiring higher level indigenous expertise. The college's core mandate is to train middle-level manpower in areas of Fisheries Technology (freshwater, brackish and marine), including aquaculture, fish processing, fishing gear design and construction, and to train deep-sea fishermen to handle dermasal and pelagic ships, alongside interwoven training of maritime professionals (traditional seafarers and shore managers) supporting national and international maritime trade based on International Maritime Organisation (IMO) Safer Shipping and Cleaner Ocean principles. Specific functions include: (a) to provide tertiary-level training in Fisheries Technology, Marine Engineering and Nautical Science through National Diploma (ND) and Higher National Diploma (HND) programmes; (b) to train fisheries technicians and technologists in freshwater, brackish water and marine fisheries; (c) to provide aquaculture technology training including fish farming and hatchery management; (d) to train personnel in fish processing, preservation, packaging and storage; (e) to provide fishing gear design, construction and maintenance training; (f) to train deep-sea fishermen for dermasal and pelagic fishing operations; (g) to provide Maritime Transport and Business Management education; (h) to train marine engineers and nautical science professionals; (i) to train industrial and labour relations personnel for maritime sector; (j) to conduct research in fisheries and marine technology; and (k) to provide consultancy services in fisheries and maritime fields. Supervised by Federal Ministry of Agriculture through Agricultural Research Council of Nigeria (ARCN), the college has benefited from institutional support from FAO and JICA, with graduates dominating shipping and industrial fishing sub-sectors.

The college's core mandate is to produce men and women with scientific knowledge necessary to rebuild the population of threatened forest resources of the country through appropriate management technology, and to train technicians and technologists for agricultural, wood, forest and environmental services. Specific functions include: (a) to provide specialized training in forestry technology through National Diploma and Higher National Diploma programmes; (b) to train forestry technicians and technologists in sustainable forest management appropriate for plateau and savannah ecosystems; (c) to provide education and training in environmental management, watershed management and soil conservation for highland areas; (d) to train personnel in forest resource assessment, inventory and monitoring for diverse ecosystems; (e) to provide training in agroforestry systems, plantation forestry and farm forestry suitable for Middle Belt region; (f) to educate students in wildlife management, rangeland management and biodiversity conservation in savannah and montane ecosystems; (g) to train personnel in wood science, timber technology and forest products processing; (h) to provide training in forest nursery management, tree breeding and selection of species suitable for highland and savannah zones; (i) to educate students in forest protection, fire management and pest control; (j) to train personnel in community forestry, extension services and participatory forest management; (k) to conduct applied research in forestry and environmental management for plateau and Middle Belt ecosystems; (l) to provide training in land degradation control, erosion management and environmental restoration; and (m) to award certificates preparing graduates for National Youth Service Corps mobilization and professional forestry careers.

## 120. FEDERAL COLLEGE OF FRESH WATER FISHERIES - BAGA

**MDA Code:** 254010001

The Federal College of Freshwater Fisheries Technology Baga, Borno State, was established by Federal Government of Nigeria under Lake Chad Research Institute in 1977 as a monotechnic institution to provide middle-level manpower in fisheries for Lake Chad basin. Originally established by proxy of Research Institute Order of 1975 (Supplement to Official Gazette No. 61, Vol. 62 of 11th December 1975 Part B) which established Lake Chad Research Institute as parent institute, the college is now under supervision of National Institute for Freshwater Fisheries Research (NIFFR), New Bussa, Niger State, following major reorganization of research institutes nationwide between 1988-1989. Located along Lake Chad shores in Baga, characterized by highly productive lake with historical irrational exploitation and inadequate knowledge of harvesting, preservation, processing and storage, the college admitted first students in 1979 for Vocational Competency in Fisheries, with immediate view of upgrading to OND and eventually HND. National Board for Technical Education halted further OND admission in 1989 pending appreciable upgrade of facilities and personnel, regularized with approval to mount Pre-ND (2008), ND (2010), and HND (2013) programmes in Fisheries Technology. The college's core mandate is to produce middle and lower level manpower in fisheries including fisheries artisans, aquaculture assistants, fisheries technicians and fisheries technologists capable of applying modern fisheries technology to boost fish production on sustainable level. Specific functions include: (a) to train junior, intermediate and senior fisheries personnel for inland fisheries development through short and long term formal education; (b) to train and retrain middle-level manpower in aquaculture technology; (c) to encourage women participation in fisheries through post-harvest technology training; (d) to train food technicians, technologists and chemical analysts in preservation, processing, packaging, storage and marketing of food commodities and equipment; (e) to train agricultural extension technicians to carry out extension work among rural farmers and fisher folks; (f) to provide training in fish farming, hatchery management and pond construction; (g) to educate students in fish biology, aquatic ecology and limnology; (h) to train personnel in fishing gear technology, boat building and fish capture methods; and (i) to be role model for fisheries, agricultural and allied tertiary institutions developing highly intellectual, skilled, creative and self-employable individuals through modern scientific, technological, research and development methods for socio-economic and political growth toward national development goals.
