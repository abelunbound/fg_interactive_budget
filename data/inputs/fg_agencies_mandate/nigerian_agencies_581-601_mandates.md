# NIGERIAN FEDERAL AGENCY MANDATES: AGENCIES 581-601

## 581. NATIONAL AGENCY FOR FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION AND CONTROL (NAFDAC)

**MDA Code:** 521014001

National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) is premier federal regulatory agency ensuring safety and quality of food, drugs, cosmetics and chemicals. Headquartered in Abuja with zonal and state offices nationwide, operating under Federal Ministry of Health. Established in 1993, NAFDAC gained prominence under late Prof. Dora Akunyili (DG 2001-2008) who revolutionized drug safety eliminating fake drugs. Regulates pharmaceuticals, food products, cosmetics, medical devices, chemicals and packaged water. Registers and approves drugs before market authorization. Conducts laboratory testing on food and drug samples. Inspects manufacturing facilities, warehouses and retail outlets. Combats counterfeit drugs and substandard products endangering public health. Monitors advertisements of regulated products. Enforces Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) in pharmaceutical companies. Issues NAFDAC numbers certifying product registration. Destroys seized fake and expired products publicly. Operates port inspection preventing dangerous imports. Collaborates with customs, police and international drug regulatory authorities. Faces challenges from sophisticated counterfeiters, porous borders and limited laboratory capacity. Critical for public health, consumer protection and pharmaceutical industry regulation.

**Core Mandate:** To regulate and control manufacture, importation, distribution and sale of food, drugs, cosmetics and chemicals; ensure safety and quality; and protect Nigerians from fake and substandard products.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Regulate food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices and chemicals in Nigeria
(b) Register and approve pharmaceutical products before market authorization
(c) Issue NAFDAC registration numbers certifying product compliance
(d) Conduct laboratory testing on food and drug samples for quality
(e) Inspect pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities enforcing GMP standards
(f) Monitor food processing plants and packaged water factories
(g) Combat counterfeit and fake drugs endangering public health
(h) Destroy seized fake, expired and substandard products publicly
(i) Inspect warehouses, pharmacies and retail outlets for compliance
(j) Conduct port inspection preventing dangerous imports at borders
(k) Monitor and regulate product advertisements and marketing claims
(l) Enforce labeling requirements on food and drug products
(m) Conduct post-market surveillance detecting substandard products
(n) Investigate adverse drug reactions and product complaints
(o) Prosecute manufacturers and distributors of fake products
(p) Provide consumer education on product safety
(q) Accredit pharmaceutical and food testing laboratories
(r) Collaborate with customs on import inspection
(s) Coordinate with WHO and international drug regulators
(t) Advance public health and consumer protection through rigorous regulation eliminating fake drugs, ensuring food safety and maintaining pharmaceutical quality standards

## 582. NATIONAL AGENCY FOR GREAT GREEN WALL (NAGGW)

**MDA Code:** 535021001

National Agency for Great Green Wall is federal agency coordinating Nigeria's participation in Africa's Great Green Wall Initiative. Headquartered in Nigeria, operating under Federal Ministry of Environment. Great Green Wall is pan-African initiative planting trees across Sahel from Senegal to Djibouti combating desertification. Nigeria participates covering 11 frontline states affected by desertification (Sokoto, Kebbi, Zamfara, Katsina, Kano, Jigawa, Yobe, Borno, Bauchi, Gombe, Adamawa). NAGGW coordinates tree planting, land restoration and sustainable land management. Combats desertification advancing southward from Sahara Desert. Restores degraded lands and vegetation cover. Promotes agroforestry and sustainable agriculture in Sahel zone. Engages communities in tree planting and conservation. Provides alternative livelihoods reducing pressure on forests. Monitors desertification and land degradation. Collaborates with Great Green Wall Africa initiative and Sahel countries. Addresses challenges from climate change, deforestation, overgrazing and inadequate funding. Critical for combating desertification threatening northern Nigeria agriculture and livelihoods.

**Core Mandate:** To coordinate Great Green Wall Initiative in Nigeria, combat desertification, restore degraded lands, promote tree planting and support sustainable land management in Sahel zone.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Coordinate Nigeria's participation in Great Green Wall pan-African initiative
(b) Combat desertification in 11 northern frontline states
(c) Plant trees across Sahel zone creating green barrier
(d) Restore degraded lands and vegetation cover
(e) Promote agroforestry integrating trees with agriculture
(f) Engage communities in tree planting campaigns
(g) Provide alternative livelihoods reducing deforestation pressure
(h) Monitor desertification advancement from Sahara Desert
(i) Conduct land degradation assessments
(j) Promote sustainable land management practices
(k) Support sustainable agriculture in semi-arid regions
(l) Address overgrazing destroying vegetation
(m) Promote soil conservation and erosion control
(n) Develop community woodlots and forest reserves
(o) Conduct environmental education on desertification
(p) Collaborate with Great Green Wall Africa Secretariat
(q) Coordinate with Sahel countries on cross-border initiatives
(r) Access climate finance for land restoration
(s) Monitor climate change impacts on northern ecosystems
(t) Advance environmental sustainability and climate resilience combating desertification threatening agriculture, water resources and livelihoods in northern Nigeria's Sahel zone

## 587. NATIONAL AGRICULTURAL INSURANCE CORPORATION (NAIC)
