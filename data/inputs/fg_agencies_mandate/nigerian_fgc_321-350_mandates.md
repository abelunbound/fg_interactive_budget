# NIGERIAN FEDERAL AGENCY MANDATES: AGENCIES 321-350 (FEDERAL GOVERNMENT COLLEGES)

## 321. FGC MINNA

**MDA Code:** 517026022

Federal Government College Minna, Niger State, is a federal unity school located in Minna, Niger State capital in North-Central Nigeria. Established as Unity School, the school provides secondary education promoting national integration. Operating under Federal Ministry of Education, FGC Minna admits students from across Nigeria through National Common Entrance Examination bringing together diverse ethnic and religious backgrounds.

**Core Mandate:** To provide quality secondary education promoting national unity and academic excellence in Niger State.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Provide secondary education from JSS1 to SS3 for students aged 10-18 years
(b) Admit students through National Common Entrance Examination ensuring national spread
(c) Implement 60% merit-based and 40% quota-based admission system
(d) Promote national unity by bringing together students from all states and ethnic groups
(e) Provide boarding facilities accommodating students from across Nigeria
(f) Deliver Nigerian national curriculum prescribed by Federal Ministry of Education
(g) Prepare students for WAEC (WASSCE) and NECO (SSCE) examinations
(h) Provide co-curricular activities including sports, cultural activities and clubs
(i) Maintain high academic standards as Unity School
(j) Employ qualified teachers meeting federal service requirements
(k) Provide science laboratories, libraries and educational infrastructure
(l) Foster inter-ethnic understanding, tolerance and national cohesion among students
(m) Develop students' leadership skills through prefect systems and student government
(n) Provide moral, civic and character education
(o) Maintain discipline and conducive learning environment
(p) Provide healthcare services through school clinic
(q) Implement school feeding programme for boarding students
(r) Organize inter-house sports and academic competitions
(s) Prepare students for university entrance examinations (JAMB/UTME)
(t) Produce well-rounded graduates contributing to national development and unity
