## 622. NATIONAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY (NEMA)

**MDA Code:** 554002001

National Emergency Management Agency is federal agency coordinating disaster management, emergency response and humanitarian assistance. Headquartered in Abuja with zonal and operational offices nationwide, operating under Office of President (previously under Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs). Established to coordinate disaster response, prevention and mitigation. Responds to natural disasters including floods, erosion and droughts. Manages response to humanitarian emergencies from conflicts and displacement. Coordinates relief distribution to disaster-affected populations. Operates IDP camps and emergency shelters. Provides search and rescue services during disasters. Conducts disaster risk reduction and preparedness programs. Collaborates with state emergency management agencies (SEMAs). Coordinates international humanitarian assistance and donor support. Responds to annual flooding affecting millions (2012 floods displaced 2+ million, 2022 floods affected 4+ million across 34 states). Addresses fire disasters, building collapses and transportation accidents. Faces challenges from climate change increasing disaster frequency, inadequate early warning systems and limited resources. Critical for disaster response, humanitarian assistance and protecting Nigerians from recurrent emergencies especially devastating annual floods.

**Core Mandate:** To coordinate disaster management and emergency response, provide humanitarian assistance, conduct disaster preparedness, mitigate disasters and protect Nigerians from emergencies and natural disasters.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Coordinate disaster management and emergency response in Nigeria
(b) Respond to natural disasters including floods and erosion
(c) Manage humanitarian emergencies from conflicts and insurgency
(d) Coordinate relief distribution to disaster-affected populations
(e) Provide food, shelter and non-food items to displaced persons
(f) Operate IDP camps and emergency shelters
(g) Provide search and rescue services during disasters
(h) Respond to annual flooding affecting millions of Nigerians
(i) Address fire disasters and building collapses
(j) Respond to transportation accidents and mass casualty incidents
(k) Conduct disaster risk reduction programs
(l) Promote disaster preparedness and early warning systems
(m) Collaborate with State Emergency Management Agencies (SEMAs)
(n) Coordinate Local Emergency Management Agencies (LEMAs)
(o) Coordinate international humanitarian assistance
(p) Manage donor support and relief supplies
(q) Conduct damage and needs assessments after disasters
(r) Promote climate change adaptation reducing disaster vulnerability
(s) Conduct public education on disaster preparedness
(t) Advance disaster resilience and emergency response managing recurrent flooding (2012: 2M displaced, 2022: 4M+ affected) and humanitarian crises protecting Nigerians from disasters
