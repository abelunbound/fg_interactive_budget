## 881. UPPER NIGER RIVER BASIN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY

**MDA Code:** 252048001

Upper Niger River Basin Development Authority manages water resources in Upper Niger basin. Operates under Federal Ministry of Water Resources as one of 12 RBDAs. Covers northwestern states including parts of Niger, Kaduna, Kano and Katsina. Develops irrigation schemes. Operates dams and reservoirs. Manages Niger River upper catchment.

**Core Mandate:** To manage Upper Niger basin water resources, develop irrigation, control floods and support agriculture in northwestern Nigeria.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Manage Upper Niger river basin water resources
(b) Develop irrigation schemes in northwestern states
(c) Operate in Niger State areas
(d) Operate in Kaduna State areas
(e) Operate in Kano State areas
(f) Operate in Katsina State areas
(g) Provide water for agriculture
(h) Provide water for livestock
(i) Provide water for domestic use
(j) Develop dry season farming through irrigation
(k) Support rice production
(l) Support wheat production
(m) Support vegetable production
(n) Operate dams and reservoirs in basin
(o) Control flooding in Niger River catchments
(p) Provide water supply infrastructure for communities
(q) Support pastoral livestock watering
(r) Manage river basin environmental protection
(s) Conduct water resources assessment and planning
(t) Advance northwestern water management supporting agriculture through irrigation development and Niger basin management
