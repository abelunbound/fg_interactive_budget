## 201. FEDERAL POLYTECHNIC DAMATURU

**MDA Code:** 517018015

The Federal Polytechnic Damaturu, Yobe State, is a federal tertiary institution providing technical and vocational education in North-East Nigeria. Located in Damaturu, Yobe State capital, the Polytechnic serves a region significantly affected by Boko Haram insurgency, making technical education crucial for reconstruction and youth engagement. Established to expand access to polytechnic education in Yobe State and provide alternative to university education, the institution addresses middle-level manpower needs for the state's agricultural, commercial and emerging industrial sectors. Operating under Federal Ministry of Education and NBTE regulation, the Polytechnic contributes to post-conflict reconstruction through skills development.

**Core Mandate:** To provide quality technical and vocational education producing skilled technicians and technologists for Yobe State and North-East Nigeria's development and reconstruction.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Provide National Diploma and Higher National Diploma programmes in NBTE-accredited disciplines
(b) Offer engineering technology programmes including electrical, mechanical, civil and computer engineering
(c) Provide agricultural technology programmes supporting Yobe's predominantly agricultural economy
(d) Offer business studies programmes including accountancy, business administration, banking and office management
(e) Provide applied science programmes including mathematics, statistics, computer science and science laboratory technology
(f) Offer environmental studies programmes including building technology, surveying and architecture
(g) Conduct Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme for practical industry training
(h) Maintain technical workshops, laboratories and agricultural demonstration facilities
(i) Award National Diploma certificates for two-year post-secondary programmes
(j) Award Higher National Diploma certificates for two-year advanced programmes beyond ND
(k) Conduct examinations and continuous assessments following NBTE guidelines
(l) Provide technical consultancy to government agencies, businesses and farmers
(m) Conduct applied research addressing regional development and reconstruction challenges
(n) Offer continuing education and skills upgrading programmes for post-conflict youth
(o) Establish entrepreneurship development programs for graduate self-employment
(p) Provide ICT training and digital literacy programmes
(q) Collaborate with reconstruction agencies for student training and employment
(r) Maintain quality assurance through NBTE accreditation compliance
(s) Provide student welfare including security, accommodation and health services
(t) Engage in peacebuilding and community development through educational outreach
