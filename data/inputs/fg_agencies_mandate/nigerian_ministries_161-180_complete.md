# NIGERIAN FEDERAL MINISTRY MANDATES: AGENCIES 161-180

## 161. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF ARTS, CULTURE AND CREATIVE ECONOMY - HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 256001001

The Federal Ministry of Art, Culture, Tourism and Creative Economy (FMACTCE) was established on August 21, 2023, by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, carved out from the Federal Ministry of Information and Culture. On October 23, 2024, it was consolidated through merger with the Federal Ministry of Tourism. The Ministry is led by Hannatu Musa Musawa as Minister. The Ministry launched "Destination 2030, Nigeria Everywhere" on November 21, 2023, targeting 10% GDP contribution from creative sector by 2030 and creation of 2 million jobs by 2027. The Ministry oversees 13 agencies including National Theatre (renamed Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and Creative Arts in July 2024), National Gallery of Arts, National Commission for Museums and Monuments, National Institute for Cultural Orientation, National Council for Arts and Culture, and Nigerian Copyright Commission.

**Core Mandate:** To promote and develop arts, culture, creative industries and tourism, using creativity to boost economy, preserve cultural heritage and share Nigeria's culture globally.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Create enabling environment encouraging artistic excellence, promoting diversity and creativity
(b) Preserve Nigeria's rich cultural heritage across 371 tribes and over 250 ethnic groups
(c) Facilitate economic diversification through creative economy, wealth creation and value addition
(d) Implement Destination 2030 initiative targeting $100 billion GDP increase by 2030
(e) Develop and implement National Tourism Policy and National Tourism Masterplan
(f) Promote public-private partnerships for tourism infrastructure development
(g) Enhance tourist sites, train professional tour guides and promote community-based tourism
(h) Support artists, cultural groups and creative businesses
(i) Position Nigeria as premier destination for cultural tourism
(j) Renovate and maintain cultural institutions including National Theatre and cultural centers
(k) Promote Nigerian music, film (Nollywood), fashion, literature and visual arts globally
(l) Protect intellectual property rights of Nigerian creatives through Nigerian Copyright Commission
(m) Organize cultural festivals, exhibitions and events showcasing Nigeria's heritage
(n) Develop creative hubs and incubation centers supporting creative entrepreneurs
(o) Collaborate with state governments, private sector and international partners

## 162. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF AVIATION AND AEROSPACE DEVELOPMENT - HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 230001001

The Federal Ministry of Aviation and Aerospace Development regulates air travel and aviation services in Nigeria. The Ministry was renamed from Federal Ministry of Aviation to include "Aerospace Development" reflecting expanded mandate. Current Minister is Festus Keyamo, CON, SAN, FCIArb (UK), appointed August 16, 2023. Permanent Secretary is Dr. Ibrahim Abubakar Kana, mni. Headquarters located at Federal Secretariat Complex Phase 1, Abuja. The Ministry operates under Five-Point Agenda: (1) ensuring strict compliance with safety regulations and continuous upward movement of Nigeria's ICAO rating; (2) supporting growth of local airline businesses; (3) infrastructure improvement; (4) human capacity development; (5) revenue optimization. The Ministry oversees Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA), Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria (FAAN), Nigerian Airspace Management Agency (NAMA), Nigerian College of Aviation Technology (NCAT) Zaria, Accident Investigation Bureau (AIB), Nigerian Meteorological Agency (NiMet), and African Aviation and Aerospace University (AAAU).

**Core Mandate:** To build safe, secured and efficient aviation industry making Nigeria hub meeting international standards and best practices for African continent.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate and manage government policies and programmes on aviation and aerospace development
(b) Oversee air transportation including licensing, regulations and operational standards for airlines
(c) Oversee airport development and management through FAAN
(d) Provide aviation infrastructural services including runways, terminals, navigation aids
(e) Provide aviation security services meeting international standards
(f) Improve airspace management and air traffic control through automation and next-generation systems
(g) Ensure safety and security measures comply with ICAO standards and WMO requirements
(h) Regulate civil aviation operations ensuring adherence to safety protocols
(i) Develop and maintain meteorological services for aviation through NiMet
(j) Investigate aircraft accidents and incidents through AIB
(k) Develop human capacity through Nigerian College of Aviation Technology
(l) Certify aerodrome emergency training schools and firefighting facilities
(m) Support growth and sustainability of local airline businesses
(n) Optimize revenue generation from aviation sector
(o) Modernize airport terminals and expand cargo and logistics networks
(p) Position Nigeria as leading aviation hub in Africa
(q) Develop aerospace education and research through African Aviation and Aerospace University

(i) Oversee National Bureau of Statistics for data generation and analysis
(j) Coordinate donor-funded projects and development partner relationships
(k) Manage Nigeria's development cooperation with bilateral and multilateral partners

(m) Coordinate implementation of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in Nigeria

(g) Promote adult and non-formal education through National Commission for Mass Literacy
(h) Oversee special education for persons with disabilities
(i) Coordinate basic education programmes through Universal Basic Education Commission (UBEC)
(j) Regulate examinations and certifications through examination bodies
(k) Develop curriculum frameworks and approve curricula for federal institutions
(l) Ensure quality assurance in education through monitoring and evaluation
(m) Coordinate teacher education and professional development
(n) Manage scholarship programmes including Federal Government scholarships and bursaries
(o) Promote educational research and development
(p) Coordinate educational infrastructure development in federal institutions
(q) Facilitate access, equity and inclusion in education

(t) Coordinate education sector development plans and budgets

(h) Manage erosion control and watershed management programmes
(i) Coordinate forest resources management and conservation through FRIN
(j) Promote afforestation and reforestation programmes
(k) Manage biodiversity conservation and protected areas
(l) Coordinate pollution control (air, water, soil) and waste management
(m) Regulate and respond to oil spills through NOSDRA
(n) Coordinate environmental emergencies and disasters response

(i) Oversee capital market development through Securities and Exchange Commission
(j) Regulate financial reporting and corporate governance
(k) Manage government investments and shareholdings
(l) Coordinate fiscal federalism and revenue allocation to states and local governments
(m) Administer Excess Crude Account and sovereign wealth funds
(n) Manage foreign exchange reserves and exchange rate policies
(o) Coordinate pension fund management and administration
(p) Regulate microfinance and financial inclusion programmes
(q) Oversee insurance industry regulation through NAICOM
(r) Manage government accounting and financial reporting standards
(s) Coordinate public procurement policies
(t) Promote investment climate reforms and ease of doing business
(u) Ensure macroeconomic stability and counter-cyclical fiscal policies

(f) Manage public health programmes including immunization, malaria control, TB control, HIV/AIDS
(g) Implement National Health Insurance Scheme promoting universal health coverage
(h) Coordinate reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health programmes
(i) Manage nutrition interventions and food security programmes
(j) Regulate medical and health professions through professional councils
(k) Coordinate health workforce development and training
(l) Manage health infrastructure development in federal health facilities
(m) Coordinate emergency medical services and disaster health response
(n) Implement International Health Regulations compliance and global health security
(o) Coordinate social welfare programmes for vulnerable groups
(p) Manage social protection schemes and safety net programmes
(q) Coordinate humanitarian response for internally displaced persons and refugees

## 175. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF LIVESTOCK DEVELOPMENT

The creation of standalone Livestock Ministry represents significant policy shift recognizing livestock sector's economic potential and need for dedicated attention to pastoralism challenges including nomadic herding, grazing routes, cattle rustling, and conflicts between farmers and herders that have caused thousands of deaths. Led by Minister of Livestock Development appointed October 2024. The Ministry is expected to coordinate livestock production, animal health services, ranching development, dairy industry, meat production, pastoral development and implementation of National Livestock Transformation Plan.

**Core Mandate:** To develop and regulate livestock sector, promote modern livestock production systems, resolve farmer-herder conflicts and enhance livestock contribution to national economy and food security.

(c) Develop and implement National Livestock Transformation Plan
(d) Establish livestock grazing reserves and ranches nationwide
(e) Coordinate ranching development and livestock settlements
(f) Resolve farmer-herder conflicts through sustainable grazing solutions
(g) Regulate nomadic pastoralism and promote sedentary livestock rearing
(h) Develop cattle colonies and grazing routes
(i) Promote dairy industry development and milk production
(j) Coordinate meat production, slaughterhouses and meat processing
(k) Oversee animal health services and veterinary services
(l) Coordinate disease control in livestock including vaccination programmes
(m) Regulate livestock trade and cattle markets
(n) Combat cattle rustling and livestock theft
(o) Promote livestock insurance schemes
(p) Develop livestock breeding programmes and genetic improvement
(q) Coordinate feeds and fodder production
(r) Promote poultry production and egg production
(s) Develop small ruminants (goats and sheep) production
(t) Coordinate livestock research through research institutes

## 176. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF MARINE AND BLUE ECONOMY - HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 254001001

The Federal Ministry of Marine and Blue Economy is newly created ministry established in August 2023 by President Bola Tinubu to harness Nigeria's maritime resources, develop blue economy and maximize economic potential of Nigeria's 853km coastline, territorial waters, Exclusive Economic Zone and inland waterways. Previously maritime affairs were handled by Federal Ministry of Transportation. The Ministry coordinates development of ocean economy including fisheries, aquaculture, shipping, offshore oil and gas, marine tourism, seabed mining and marine biotechnology. Led by Minister of Marine and Blue Economy appointed 2023 assisted by Minister of State and Permanent Secretary. The Ministry coordinates Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency (NIMASA), Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA), National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA), and implements Cabotage Act promoting indigenous shipping.

**Core Mandate:** To develop and regulate marine resources, promote sustainable blue economy development, ensure maritime security and maximize economic benefits from Nigeria's maritime domain.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate and implement national marine and blue economy policies
(b) Develop blue economy master plan harnessing maritime resources
(c) Coordinate maritime transport and shipping industry development
(d) Oversee Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency (NIMASA) for shipping regulation
(e) Promote indigenous shipping and cabotage regime implementation
(f) Regulate ports and port operations through Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA)
(g) Develop port infrastructure and maritime logistics
(h) Coordinate inland waterways development through National Inland Waterways Authority
(i) Promote maritime security and combat piracy, sea robbery and illegal fishing
(j) Coordinate marine fisheries development and aquaculture
(k) Regulate offshore oil and gas operations in territorial waters
(l) Promote seabed mining and marine mineral resources exploitation
(m) Develop marine tourism and coastal tourism
(n) Regulate maritime labour and seafarers welfare
(o) Promote ship building and ship repair industry
(p) Coordinate maritime education and training through maritime academies
(q) Manage maritime safety and search and rescue operations
(r) Promote marine environmental protection and ocean conservation
(s) Coordinate hydrographic surveys and nautical charting
(t) Maximize revenue generation from maritime sector

## 177. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF NIGER DELTA HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 451001001

The Federal Ministry of Niger Delta Affairs was established in 2008 during President Umaru Yar'Adua's administration to address decades of neglect, environmental degradation, youth restiveness, militancy and underdevelopment in oil-rich Niger Delta region comprising nine oil-producing states: Abia, Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa, Cross River, Delta, Edo, Imo, Ondo and Rivers. Despite contributing over 80% of Nigeria's foreign exchange earnings, the region suffered environmental degradation from oil pollution and gas flaring, poverty, unemployment and insecurity. Led by Minister of Niger Delta Affairs assisted by Minister of State and Permanent Secretary, the Ministry coordinates special interventions complementing Niger Delta Development Commission (NDDC). The Ministry implements amnesty programmes for ex-militants, coordinates environmental remediation including Ogoniland cleanup, and facilitates peace building in oil-producing communities.

**Core Mandate:** To coordinate development programmes and projects in Niger Delta region, facilitate peace building, environmental restoration and socio-economic development.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate and implement special development policies for Niger Delta region
(b) Coordinate Federal Government interventions in Niger Delta complementing NDDC
(c) Facilitate infrastructure development including roads, bridges, electricity, water supply
(d) Coordinate environmental remediation and cleanup of oil-impacted sites
(e) Implement amnesty programmes for ex-militants and restive youths
(f) Promote youth empowerment and vocational training for Niger Delta youths
(g) Coordinate scholarships and educational support for Niger Delta students
(h) Facilitate conflict resolution and peace building in oil-producing communities
(i) Promote community development and grassroots empowerment
(j) Coordinate poverty alleviation interventions targeting Niger Delta poor
(k) Facilitate host community relations between oil companies and communities
(l) Address grievances regarding resource control and fiscal federalism
(m) Coordinate health interventions addressing health challenges in riverine areas
(n) Promote agricultural development and fisheries in Niger Delta
(o) Coordinate resettlement programmes for displaced communities
(p) Facilitate women empowerment programmes
(q) Coordinate maritime security in Niger Delta waters
(r) Monitor compliance with environmental standards by oil companies
(s) Coordinate disaster response in flood-prone riverine communities
(t) Facilitate economic diversification beyond oil in Niger Delta

## 178. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF POLICE AFFAIRS

**MDA Code:** 155001001

The Federal Ministry of Police Affairs was established in 1999 when President Olusegun Obasanjo separated police affairs from Interior Ministry. The Ministry is responsible for police policy formulation, police welfare, budget management, logistics and support services for Nigeria Police Force, while operational control remains with Inspector-General of Police under Police Act 2020. Led by Minister of Police Affairs assisted by Minister of State and Permanent Secretary, the Ministry serves as liaison between Federal Government and Nigeria Police Force, providing policy guidance and administrative support without interfering in operational command structure. The Ministry manages police budget, procures equipment, develops police infrastructure including barracks and training facilities, and coordinates police reforms.

**Core Mandate:** To formulate policies for effective policing, provide logistical support to Nigeria Police Force, manage police budget and ensure police welfare.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate national police policies and strategies
(b) Prepare and manage Nigeria Police Force annual budget
(c) Provide logistics and equipment for police operations
(d) Procure vehicles, communications equipment, arms and ammunition for police
(e) Manage police infrastructure development including barracks, police stations, training facilities
(f) Coordinate police welfare including salaries, allowances, pensions and insurance
(g) Facilitate police training and capacity building programmes
(h) Coordinate police academy and police colleges development
(i) Promote police-community relations and community policing
(j) Facilitate police reform initiatives and police modernization
(k) Coordinate implementation of Police Act and regulations
(l) Liaise between Federal Government and Inspector-General of Police
(m) Process police promotions and appointments for Federal Executive Council approval
(n) Manage police housing and accommodation schemes
(o) Coordinate police healthcare and medical services
(p) Facilitate police pensions and retirement benefits
(q) Promote police professionalism and ethics
(r) Coordinate human rights training for police officers
(s) Coordinate international cooperation and technical assistance for police
(t) Promote gender mainstreaming and women in policing

## 179. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF POWER -HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 231001001

The Federal Ministry of Power is responsible for formulating and implementing policies for power sector development, electricity generation, transmission, distribution, rural electrification and renewable energy. Following Electric Power Sector Reform Act 2005 which liberalized sector and created successor companies to defunct National Electric Power Authority (NEPA/PHCN), the Ministry emerged focusing exclusively on power sector. Led by Minister of Power assisted by Minister of State and Permanent Secretary. The Ministry supervises Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC), Rural Electrification Agency (REA), Transmission Company of Nigeria (TCN), and coordinates with privatized generation companies (GenCos) and distribution companies (DisCos). The Ministry implements national grid expansion, promotes renewable energy including solar and wind, coordinates gas-to-power initiatives, and facilitates Nigeria's participation in West African Power Pool.

**Core Mandate:** To ensure availability of reliable, adequate and affordable electricity for all Nigerians, promote electricity access and facilitate power sector development.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate and implement national power policies and electricity sector policies
(b) Coordinate electricity generation capacity expansion through thermal, hydro, solar and renewable
(c) Oversee transmission infrastructure development and grid expansion through TCN
(d) Regulate electricity sector through Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission
(e) Coordinate rural electrification programmes through Rural Electrification Agency
(f) Promote renewable energy development including solar, wind, biomass and mini-grids
(g) Facilitate electricity distribution improvements and reduce technical and commercial losses
(h) Coordinate electricity tariff reviews and cost-reflective tariffs
(i) Promote electricity access for off-grid and underserved communities
(j) Facilitate independent power projects (IPPs) and embedded generation
(k) Coordinate power sector reforms and privatization outcomes
(l) Manage federal government-owned generation assets
(m) Promote gas-to-power initiatives leveraging Nigeria's gas resources
(n) Coordinate regional electricity trade through West African Power Pool
(o) Facilitate electricity infrastructure financing and investments
(p) Promote electricity metering and reduce estimated billing
(q) Coordinate demand-side management and energy efficiency programmes
(r) Promote electricity safety standards and regulations
(s) Coordinate electricity market development and Nigerian Electricity Supply Industry
(t) Manage national grid stability and reduce system collapses

## 180. FEDERAL MINISTRY OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION - HQTRS

**MDA Code:** 228001001

The Federal Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (renamed from Federal Ministry of Science and Technology to emphasize Innovation under President Buhari's second term, continued by President Tinubu) is responsible for formulating and implementing policies promoting scientific research, technological development, innovation and technology transfer for national development. Led by Minister of Science, Technology and Innovation assisted by Minister of State and Permanent Secretary. The Ministry supervises over 35 research institutes and agencies including National Space Research and Development Agency (NASRDA), Energy Commission of Nigeria, National Office for Technology Acquisition and Promotion (NOTAP), Raw Materials Research and Development Council, National Agency for Science and Engineering Infrastructure (NASENI), National Biotechnology Development Agency (NABDA), Nigerian Building and Road Research Institute (NBRRI), Federal Institute of Industrial Research Oshodi (FIIRO), and numerous sector-specific research institutes.

**Core Mandate:** To harness science, technology and innovation for economic development, industrialization and improving quality of life.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Formulate and implement national science, technology and innovation policies
(b) Coordinate scientific research across research institutes and universities
(c) Promote research and development in biotechnology, nanotechnology, ICT, renewable energy
(d) Oversee National Space Research and Development Agency for space science and satellite technology
(e) Coordinate Energy Commission of Nigeria for nuclear science and renewable energy research
(f) Regulate technology acquisition and transfer through NOTAP
(g) Promote raw materials research and development for local utilization
(h) Coordinate engineering infrastructure development through NASENI
(i) Promote biotechnology development and biosafety regulation
(j) Coordinate science and technology education and STEM promotion
(k) Manage science and technology parks, innovation hubs and incubation centers
(l) Promote commercialization of research outputs and intellectual property
(m) Coordinate technology innovation and entrepreneurship development
(n) Facilitate public-private partnerships in research and development
(o) Promote indigenous technology development and local content
(p) Coordinate metrology, standards and quality infrastructure
(q) Promote agricultural research, industrial research and health research
(r) Coordinate climate change research and environmental technologies
(s) Manage national research fund and research grants administration
(t) Develop national innovation system and innovation ecosystems
