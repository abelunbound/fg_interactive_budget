## 681. NATIONAL SALARIES, INCOMES AND WAGES COMMISSION (NSIWC)

**MDA Code:** 242001001

National Salaries, Incomes and Wages Commission is federal agency regulating public sector salaries, wages and remuneration. Operating under Federal Ministry of Finance. Established to advise government on salaries policy, negotiate with labor unions and ensure equitable public sector remuneration. Advises Federal Government on salaries and wages policy. Negotiates salaries with labor unions (NLC, TUC). Determines salary structures for federal civil servants. Reviews and recommends minimum wage adjustments. Conducts salary surveys and remuneration studies. Ensures salary equity across MDAs. Addresses salary disparities and harmonization. Negotiates with ASUU on university lecturers' salaries (frequent strikes). Handles labor disputes on remuneration issues. Promotes productivity-linked pay systems. Collaborates with states on salary standardization. Addresses challenges from limited government revenues constraining salary increases, labor unions' demands for higher pay, ASUU strikes over poor remuneration and inflation eroding real wages. Public sector wage bill consumes significant budget portion. Minimum wage increased to N30,000 (2019) but implementation incomplete in states. Critical for industrial harmony, fair remuneration and balancing workers' welfare with fiscal sustainability.

**Core Mandate:** To regulate public sector salaries and wages, advise on remuneration policy, negotiate with labor unions, ensure equitable pay and promote industrial harmony.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Advise Federal Government on salaries, wages and remuneration policy
(b) Negotiate salaries and wages with labor unions (NLC, TUC)
(c) Determine salary structures for federal civil servants
(d) Review and recommend minimum wage adjustments
(e) Implement national minimum wage policy (currently N30,000)
(f) Conduct salary surveys across public sector
(g) Ensure salary equity and harmonization across MDAs
(h) Address salary disparities between similar positions
(i) Negotiate with ASUU on university lecturers' remuneration
(j) Handle labor disputes on salary and allowances
(k) Review pension and gratuity policies
(l) Promote productivity-linked pay and performance-based remuneration
(m) Develop job grading and classification systems
(n) Collaborate with states on salary standardization
(o) Address public sector wage bill sustainability
(p) Balance workers' welfare with fiscal constraints
(q) Research best practices in public sector remuneration
(r) Monitor inflation impact on real wages
(s) Address ASUU and other unions' strike actions
(t) Advance industrial harmony through fair remuneration balancing workers' welfare demands with government fiscal capacity despite limited revenues and recurring labor disputes

## 682. OFFICE OF THE NATIONAL SECURITY ADVISER (ONSA)

**MDA Code:** 157001001

Office of the National Security Adviser is apex national security coordination body advising President on security matters. Located in Abuja, operating directly under Presidency. National Security Adviser appointed by President coordinates Nigeria's security architecture. Advises President on national security policy and strategy. Coordinates security agencies (military, police, DSS, NIA, NSCDC). Develops national security strategy addressing terrorism, insurgency and threats. Coordinates counter-terrorism operations against Boko Haram and ISWAP. Oversees National Counter Terrorism Centre operations. Manages Multinational Joint Task Force (MNJTF) coordination. Coordinates intelligence sharing among security agencies. Addresses security challenges - Boko Haram insurgency (35,000+ deaths), northwestern banditry, kidnapping epidemic, farmer-herder conflicts, IPOB agitations, oil theft. Coordinates military operations in northeast, northwest and other theaters. Oversees defense procurement and security equipment acquisition. Manages security budgets and funding allocation. Collaborates with international security partners. Current NSA coordinates whole-of-government security response. Faces challenges from multiple simultaneous security crises, inter-agency coordination difficulties and resource constraints. Critical for national security coordination, strategic planning and managing Nigeria's complex multi-dimensional security threats.

**Core Mandate:** To advise President on national security, coordinate security agencies, develop security strategy, manage counter-terrorism operations and ensure effective national security response.

**Statutory Functions:**
(a) Advise President on national security policy and strategy
(b) Coordinate Nigeria's security agencies and architecture
(c) Coordinate military, police, DSS, NIA and other security services
(d) Develop national security strategy addressing threats
(e) Coordinate counter-terrorism operations against Boko Haram and ISWAP
(f) Oversee National Counter Terrorism Centre (NCTC) operations
(g) Manage Multinational Joint Task Force (MNJTF) against Boko Haram
(h) Coordinate intelligence sharing among security agencies
(i) Address Boko Haram insurgency (35,000+ deaths since 2009)
(j) Coordinate response to northwestern banditry and kidnapping
(k) Address farmer-herder conflicts and communal violence
(l) Manage IPOB separatist agitations and southeastern security
(m) Coordinate military operations across security theaters
(n) Oversee defense procurement and security equipment acquisition
(o) Manage security budgets and resource allocation
(p) Coordinate border security with neighboring countries
(q) Address oil theft and pipeline vandalism in Niger Delta
(r) Collaborate with US, UK, France and international security partners
(s) Promote inter-agency cooperation and information sharing
(t) Advance national security through strategic coordination managing multiple simultaneous crises (insurgency, banditry, kidnapping) ensuring coherent whole-of-government security response
